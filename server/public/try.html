Drop in work

Hundreds of picks and inbound pallets at once. Items, customers, shipping lanes and delivery sizes are drawn at random from the stock that is free to ship and the rack space left. Nothing is pre-planned: the system makes every job and decides who does what, in what order.

Drivers

Every handheld on Auto drives itself: it scans what its screen asks for, with the time a reach truck takes to get there. Watch it at real speed, or faster. Untick a truck to drive it yourself.

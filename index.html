A simulated shift with yoghurt and cheese in separate aisles. Trucks on Auto get their next job the moment they finish, only in their own categories, in the order the coordinator sets, and never more than two in one aisle. Moving a pallet is two scans. Receiving reads batch, expiry, item, SSCC and quantity, and a GS1 pallet label does all five in one scan. Picks follow first expired, first out. Blocked stock never ships.

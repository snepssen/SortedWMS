A simulated shift with yoghurt and cheese in separate aisles, one-way traffic in every aisle, cheese crate pallets in block stacks, and a process floor (pallet change, crates off + foil, plate press and warm room). Trucks on Auto get their next job the moment they finish, only in their own categories, in the order the coordinator sets, and never more than two in one aisle. Moving a pallet is two scans. Receiving reads batch, best-before, item, SSCC and quantity from the supplier label, or from the bottom barcode alone with a delivery list. Picks follow first expired, first out, and nothing with fewer days left than the manager's minimum is shipped.
